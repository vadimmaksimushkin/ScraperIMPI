Copies search (Ejemplares)

area is required; pass a gaceta id, or a fecha_desde/fecha_hasta range. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.

Records search (Búsqueda en fichas)

busqueda is required (a numeric record id, ≥ 2 digits). gacetas is comma-separated and only valid together with area. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.

Advanced search (Búsqueda especializada)

(POST) area is required. Possible to add 0-N datos, api accepts only 1 or 2: operador should be blank on the first, and AND/OR/NOT after; add valor or fecha per the column. gacetas/secciones are comma-separated ids. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.
