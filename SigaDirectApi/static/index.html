Copies search

Ejemplares search. area is required; pass a gaceta id, or a fecha_desde/fecha_hasta range. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.

Records search

Fichas search. busqueda is required (a numeric record id, ≥ 2 digits). gacetas is comma-separated and only valid together with area. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.

Advanced search

Estructurada search (POST). area is required. Add 0–N datos terms: leave operador blank on the first, use AND/OR/NOT after; fill valor or fecha per the column's kind. gacetas/secciones are comma-separated ids. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.
